CASOGEOGRAPHICS — THE ROUTE LOGO SYSTEM

Concept
The route monogram draws C and G as one forward-moving system. The outer C is
the field in which a project develops; the internal G becomes a route that
leaves the frame and reaches a destination. The three accents connect LAND,
SCREEN and ARENA without using a map pin, globe, compass or generic target.

Clear space
Keep free space around the primary logo equal to at least the width of the
central violet route stroke. Do not stretch, rotate, outline, shadow or recolour
individual elements.
